## Directions for client / agency review

These are art-direction concepts, not final design decisions.

### A — Institutional warmth (recommended starting point)
Warm white / restrained deep green / muted mineral neutrals, editorial typography, confident layouts, authentic architecture and clinician photography. Mood: established, calm, locally trusted, human.

### B — Clinical precision
Deep navy / white / restrained cool accent, sharp typographic hierarchy, precise information design, minimal ornament. Mood: modern, capable, structured, medically focused.

### C — Human-centred local care
Soft ivory / sage / warm charcoal, natural light, human photography, approachable typography and clear wayfinding. Mood: welcoming, accessible, community-oriented.

Do not choose final palette until the existing logo, hospital signage, interior/exterior environment and management preferences have been reviewed.

## Design principles

- Premium editorial composition with purposeful whitespace and clear hierarchy.
- Real hospital, facility and clinician photography; no generic stock people posing as staff.
- Avoid generic medical icon packs, repetitive card grids, loud gradients and decorative motion.
- One primary CTA per section; calls and emergency information remain unambiguous.
- 8px spacing rhythm, consistent radii, type scale and responsive layout rules.
- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and focus states.
- Mobile is the primary constraint; ensure text, booking and contact flows work on modest devices and networks.
- Motion must support hierarchy, never block content, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Figma handoff

Before detailed screen design:
1. Confirm public brand name and logo.
2. Review real-world colour references (signage/interior) and direction A/B/C.
3. Confirm English/Kannada/Hindi content strategy.
4. Approve typography pair and colour tokens with contrast tests.
5. Design mobile navigation, homepage, speciality detail, doctor detail, appointment form and contact page.
6. Record component states, responsive behaviour, accessibility and motion rules.
