## Questions for the client / agency

1. What is the exact legal and preferred public hospital name?
2. Which official logo and brand assets should be used?
3. Which visual direction should be developed: A institutional warmth, B clinical precision, or C human-centred local care?
4. Confirm exact address, phone, WhatsApp, email and Google Business Profile URL.
5. Confirm current specialities, doctor roster, facilities and OPD/emergency schedules.
6. Provide current PM-JAY and insurance documents / packages if these are to be promoted.
7. Is appointment booking a callback request or a true time-slot system?
8. Who receives/handles patient requests and what is the response SLA?
9. Does the hospital need a secure staff dashboard or is an existing system used?
10. Which account owns domain, DNS, hosting, database, messaging vendors and renewals?
11. Are Kannada and Hindi pages required at launch?
12. Who is the authorised approver for medical content, privacy and launch?

## Change control

Record decisions that affect scope, cost, patient data, third-party vendors, hosting, or clinical claims here before implementation. Do not infer approval from a verbal idea or a third-party directory entry.
