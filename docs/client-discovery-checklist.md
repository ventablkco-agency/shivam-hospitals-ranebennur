# Client discovery and approval checklist

This is the information request for the hospital management meeting. Record answers and evidence before design/copy approval.

## Identity and brand

- [ ] Legal registered entity name and preferred public name.
- [ ] Exact spelling and capitalisation: Shivam Hospitals / other approved variant.
- [ ] Hospital history, opening year, key milestones and ownership/management details.
- [ ] Existing logo source files, brand guide, signage and approved colours.
- [ ] Official website, domain registrar, DNS, social profiles and Google Business Profile URL.
- [ ] Who approves copy, photographs, clinical facts and final website?

## Contact and location

- [ ] Exact postal address and PIN code.
- [ ] Google Maps pin / Plus Code / directions URL.
- [ ] Main phone, alternate phone, WhatsApp number and email.
- [ ] Which numbers are staffed and during which hours?
- [ ] General opening hours, OPD timings, visiting hours, emergency hours.
- [ ] Emergency phone, ambulance availability and service area, if any.
- [ ] Preferred contact method and who answers website leads?

## Care and clinicians

- [ ] Complete list of currently active specialities and services.
- [ ] Services/procedures not offered or referred elsewhere.
- [ ] Current doctor/clinician roster, registration and qualification evidence.
- [ ] Consultation days, timings, appointment lead time and fees (if public).
- [ ] Languages spoken and patient groups served.
- [ ] Who signs off on clinical copy and FAQs?

## Facilities and trust

- [ ] Bed count, ICU capacity, OT count, lab/pharmacy details, equipment and emergency capability—only if management wants public claims.
- [ ] Current licences, accreditation and certifications with validity/scope.
- [ ] Insurance/TPA network list and current cashless process.
- [ ] PM-JAY registration, package/speciality list and authorised scheme coordinator.
- [ ] Any awards, milestones or statistics with documentary evidence.

## Patient journey

- [ ] What is the existing appointment process?
- [ ] Does the site need callback requests, appointment slots, or only click-to-call?
- [ ] Who receives form submissions and within what response timeframe?
- [ ] What happens outside working hours?
- [ ] Cancellation, rescheduling and no-show process.
- [ ] Are existing HIS/EMR/CRM systems in use? Any integration constraints?
- [ ] Do not integrate with patient systems until access, security, scope and contracts are approved.

## Content and media

- [ ] Approved hospital overview and mission/values.
- [ ] Exterior, reception, department, facility and clinician photographs.
- [ ] Written consent and rights for all identifiable patients/staff.
- [ ] Approved Google reviews / profile link and response process.
- [ ] English, Kannada and Hindi language requirements; who reviews translations?
- [ ] FAQs from reception staff and recurring patient calls.

## Privacy and operations

- [ ] Legal entity/data fiduciary contact and grievance channel.
- [ ] Appointment form fields and precise purpose.
- [ ] Retention period, access roles, deletion request process.
- [ ] Which staff can see appointment requests? Offboarding owner?
- [ ] Approved email/SMS/WhatsApp vendors and account ownership.
- [ ] Hosting/domain account ownership, renewal and billing responsibility.
- [ ] Support owner, maintenance agreement, backup and incident contact.

## Final approvals required

- [ ] Public hospital identity, address and contact.
- [ ] Sitemap and page scope.
- [ ] Brand direction and Figma homepage.
- [ ] Every speciality, clinician, facility and scheme claim.
- [ ] Reviews and all patient imagery.
- [ ] Privacy notice, terms and appointment disclaimer.
- [ ] Booking data flow, vendors, retention and access.
- [ ] Go-live checklist and post-launch owner.
