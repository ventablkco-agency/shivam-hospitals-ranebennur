## Claims that must not appear without evidence

- “Best”, “No. 1”, “leading”, “trusted by thousands”, or similar superiority claims.
- Cure/success rates, guaranteed outcomes, treatment guarantees, or unsupported clinical outcomes.
- “24/7 emergency” or “round-the-clock specialist” without confirmed staffing and operating arrangements.
- NABH/NABL/other accreditation, awards, government approval, or certification without current proof and scope.
- Doctor experience, patient counts, bed counts, ICU/OT counts, technology claims, or years of operation without evidence.
- PM-JAY or insurance cashless claims beyond currently approved packages and policy terms.
- Any review rating/count that is not rechecked against the exact Google profile immediately before publication.

12. Photo/video releases and clinician/patient consent for all identifiable subjects.
